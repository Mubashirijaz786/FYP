## Project Overview

**Market on Wheels** is a location-based, multi-vendor marketplace that will be available through a **website and mobile application**. The basic idea of the project is to provide customers with a single platform where they can purchase products from **multiple nearby vendors and receive them through a combined delivery**.

In traditional online ordering systems, customers generally place an order from one vendor at a time. If a customer needs grocery items, medicine, milk, fruits, and food from different shops, they may need to place several separate orders and potentially pay separate delivery charges.

**Market on Wheels** aims to improve this process by allowing customers to purchase products from different nearby vendors through a **single shopping experience**.

For example, a customer may need:

- Grocery items from a grocery store
- Medicine from a pharmacy
- Milk from a dairy shop
- Fruits and vegetables from a local shop
- Food from a restaurant

Instead of placing five separate orders, the customer will be able to select the required products from different vendors and combine them into **one multi-vendor order**.

### Location-Based Marketplace

The system will use the customer's **current location** to identify relevant nearby vendors.

Rather than displaying shops that are too far away, the platform will focus on vendors that can practically serve the customer's location. This will make product discovery more relevant and can also help improve the delivery process.

Location information can be used to determine:

- Nearby vendors
- Available products in the customer's area
- Approximate delivery distance
- Delivery zones
- Delivery charges
- Suitable pickup locations

The exact rules for vendor distance and delivery zones will be refined as the project progresses.

### Multi-Vendor Ordering

A major concept behind **Market on Wheels** is the ability to create a **multi-vendor cart**.

Customers will not be restricted to purchasing from only one shop. Products from multiple nearby vendors can be added to the same order.

For example:

**Grocery Store + Pharmacy + Dairy Shop + Restaurant → One Customer Order**

The platform will organize the products according to their respective vendors while providing the customer with a single combined ordering experience.

### Combined Delivery

Another important concept of the project is **combined delivery**.

Instead of assigning separate deliveries for every vendor, the system will aim to combine multiple nearby vendor pickups into a single delivery whenever practical.

A rider may collect products from multiple vendors included in the customer's order and then deliver the complete order to the customer.

A simplified delivery flow can be:

**Customer Places Order → Multiple Vendors Prepare Items → Rider Collects Items → Customer Receives Combined Order**

This approach is intended to reduce unnecessary separate deliveries and make multi-vendor shopping more convenient for customers.

### Smart Delivery Zones

Since vendors may be located at different distances from the customer, the system will also consider **delivery zones**.

Nearby vendors within a suitable area may be included within the normal combined delivery. If a customer selects a vendor located farther away, the delivery cost may be adjusted according to the additional distance or delivery zone.

The exact delivery fee calculation model has not yet been finalized and will be developed further after discussion and evaluation during the project.

### Route Optimization

Because a combined order can involve multiple vendor locations, the rider may need to visit several pickup points before delivering the order.

The project will therefore explore **route optimization** to determine a suitable sequence for collecting products.

For example, instead of randomly visiting:

**Vendor C → Vendor A → Vendor B → Customer**

the system can evaluate the locations and determine a more efficient pickup sequence such as:

**Rider → Vendor A → Vendor B → Vendor C → Customer**

The objective will be to reduce unnecessary travel distance, delivery time, and travel cost.

### AI-Based Shopping Assistance

**Market on Wheels** will also explore the use of **AI-based shopping assistance**.

Instead of requiring customers to manually search through every shop and product, the customer may be able to describe what they need in simple language.

For example:

> "I need milk, bread, eggs, fruits and Panadol."

The system can use this request to help identify relevant products and nearby vendors and present suitable options to the customer.

AI may also assist customers in discovering products and comparing available options. However, the customer will remain responsible for reviewing and confirming the final order.

### Project Goal

The overall goal of **Market on Wheels** is to explore a smarter way of connecting customers with multiple nearby businesses through a single marketplace.

The central idea of the project can be summarized as:

**Multiple Nearby Vendors → One Shopping Experience → One Combined Order → Optimized Pickup → Single Delivery**

The project will gradually be refined during development and supervisor meetings. Additional features, technical decisions, system modules, algorithms, and implementation details will be finalized as the project progresses.
